    Hourly values (table)

    Metrics are computed on hourly day-ahead prices over realized (settled) days only. MAE is mean absolute error in €/MWh; bias is mean(simulated − actual) in €/MWh, so a negative bias means the model under-prices; corr is the Pearson correlation of hourly prices. Correlation is color-coded:  good ≥ 0.75,  0.40–0.75,  weak < 0.40. Click a column header to sort (bias sorts by distance from zero); GR stays pinned first. Hover a cell for the number of scored days.

    About these numbers

    This is a no-fit, ex-ante competitive counterfactual — a fundamentals-based market-clearing model, not a statistical forecaster. Predictions are frozen before delivery (morning runs are frozen before the day-ahead auction itself), never revised, and scored honestly as days settle. Nothing is fitted to the outcomes it is scored against, so persistent gaps between simulated and actual prices are findings about how the real market deviates from competitive behavior — not model errors to be tuned away.
