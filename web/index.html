    Metrics are computed on hourly day-ahead prices over realized (settled) days only. MAE is mean absolute error in €/MWh; bias is mean(simulated − actual) in €/MWh, so a negative bias means the model under-prices; corr is the Pearson correlation of hourly prices.     About these numbers

    This is a no-fit competitive counterfactual — a fundamentals-based market-clearing model, not a statistical forecaster. Predictions are frozen before delivery, never revised, and scored honestly as days settle. Be precise about what today's scores are: the reference track uses ENTSO-E's published D-1 forecasts and freezes in the evening — before delivery, but after the auction. The truly ex-ante track — our own weather-based wind/solar, frozen before the auction gate — is being wired in and will be scored separately. Nothing is fitted to the outcomes it is scored against, so persistent gaps between simulated and actual prices are findings about how the real market deviates from competitive behavior — not model errors to be tuned away.
