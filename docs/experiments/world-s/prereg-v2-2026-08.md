# World S v2 — unit-level conduct profiles (prereg DRAFT, 2026-08-11; freezes at merge)

Successor to the fleet-transplant NO ([pilot-v1](pilot-v1-results-2026-08.md):
the exchange book is not the market — volume bases differ by the bilateral
share). v2 keeps OUR volume base and imports only per-unit CONDUCT, the thing
Phase 0 measured as stable (67% of GME units single-shape over 18 months).
Enabler: the GME↔ENTSO-E mapping is ground truth (ENTSO-E's Italian unit
names ARE the Terna UP codes; 992 units exact, 87.7% of offered MWh).

## Mechanism

For each matched unit (mapping high+medium), a trailing profile from LAGGED
GME books (≥30 days — GME publishes with delay; lagged public books are a
declared-admissible ex-ante input):

- dominant shape (Phase-0 classes);
- for markup-shaped units: the unit's offer ladder as RATIOS to the day's
  gas SRMC (median per tranche over the trailing window) — applied as
  ratio × the CURRENT day's SRMC (ex-ante);
- for zero/negative shapes: the trailing price level;
- withheld share: the fraction of its offered MW priced >2.5× SRMC,
  re-priced to 3000 in our book.

Applied via the strategist hook to our book's matched unit orders only;
unmatched MW (RES aggregates, completion blocks, demand) untouched.

## Evaluation (frozen; both levels, per the v1 lessons)

1. **Bid-level (out-of-sample conduct transfer)**: on the 2026 test days,
   per matched unit, MW-weighted absolute price distance of (a) the
   profile-transformed ladder and (b) our SRMC ladder to the unit's ACTUAL
   observed offers that day. **Gate: profiles beat SRMC on ≥60% of matched
   MW.** (If conduct is stable, this must clear; if it fails, the program
   stops here.)
- 2. **Price-level (the money question)**: full 39-zone coupled clear
   (passes=2) of the three 2026 GME days, strategist ON for the seven IT
   zones vs OFF, scored against official IT zonal prices. **Gate: IT-7 MAE
   improves ≥2.0 with no phantom-collapse increase and standard envelope/cap
   guards on all 39.** Labels `ws2_{off,on}_<day>`; canonical record
   untouched (World-S labels only).
- Calibration days: 2025 frozen GME days (profiles); test: 2026-01-15,
  2026-04-15, 2026-07-15. Disclosed: these test days' PRICES were seen in
  the v1 iterations; the bid-level metric and the coupled-clear arms are
  new measurements. The next GME fetch can extend to untouched days if the
  owner wants a clean-room confirmation.
