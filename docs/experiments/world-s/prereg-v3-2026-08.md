# World S v3 — CONDITION-based unit profiles (prereg DRAFT, 2026-08-11; freezes at merge)

Successor to v2 ([gate-1 fail](v2-gate1-results-2026-08.md): conduct transfer
is seasonal). Owner directive (2026-08-11, verbatim intent): **no
calendar/month rule** — profiles conditioned on PHYSICAL state that is
identically computable in past and future: temperature, sun position,
daylight hours, light levels. Conditions predict the season implicitly but
also transfer OUT of season, and every condition bucket draws calibration
mass from the whole history rather than one month.

## Condition axes (all ex-ante, all already in the stack)

Per zone-hour: **sun elevation** (`se`, astronomical), **daylight length**
of the day (astronomical), **solar share of forecast load** (the standing
regime axis), **temperature** (zone-mean, the weather layer's source),
**GHI** (light intensity). Bucketing (frozen here): sun-up vs sun-down ×
solar-share terciles × temperature terciles (zone-relative, trailing-year) —
18 buckets, collapsed to their marginal where a unit-bucket has < 5
calibration days.

## Mechanism

Per matched unit × condition bucket: the v2 profile recipe (shape shares,
elastic ratio ladder r25/r50/r75 vs day SRMC, withheld share, negative
level), calibrated over ALL trailing days whose conditions fall in the
bucket (lag ≥ 30 days — lagged public books). Applied per-hour by the
DELIVERY hour's own conditions.
