# cv31 record scores — activation-record comparison vs cv27 (2026-08-01)

The cv31 record backfill (2023-01-01..2026-07-27, 1,234/1,304 usable days,
39-zone `multi_zone_eu`, HiGHS, pipelined 50+10 workers, 4.13 h) scored
against settled Day-ahead prices, side by side with the cv27 record on the
same days. Method: hourly sim (from `data/results.duckdb`,
`clearing_mode='multi_zone_eu'`, `code_version IN (27,31)`) joined to hourly
settled (`entsoe.energy_prices`, Day-ahead) from the live extract; script:
[`cv31_scoreboard.py`](cv31_scoreboard.py). Energy weights = zone-year
`actual_total_load` sums. Postgres slice verified complete after transfer
(1,155,024 rows; 1677/1677 zone-month checksums after the NUMERIC(10,2)
like-for-like fix, #260).
