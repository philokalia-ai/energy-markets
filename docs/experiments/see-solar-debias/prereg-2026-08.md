# SEE solar-forecast conditional debias — preregistration (FROZEN 2026-08-06 ~20:15Z, before the scored run)

## The measured defect (cartography, session record 2026-08-06)

The SEE false-collapse hours (sim ≤ €20 while settled ≥ €60; year census on
`trmk_base_fy`: RO 279 h, BG 232 h, GR 163 h, concentrated in high-solar
middays Mar–Jul) are dominated by an INPUT artifact: in those hours the
ENTSO-E D-1 RES forecast overshoots the realized RES by **GR +1,071 MW /
BG +619 / RO +669** on average. The real system was not in surplus — GR ran
1,248 MW of gas in those very hours and settled at €89–100 ≈ gas SRMC. The
overshoot is **persistent and conditional**: the monthly solar fc/actual
ratio in high-share hours (share_fc ≥ 0.3, fc > 200 MW) is > 1.1 in nearly
every month of the year for all three zones (GR 1.13–1.66, RO 1.30–1.59,
BG 0.93–1.82 with ≥1.1 from Feb-2026 on). Market participants demonstrably
do not clear on the TSO figure; our book price-takes it at €1.

## Treatment (inputs layer — where fitting is openly allowed)

Per zone in {GR, BG, RO}, per delivery day D: the **demonstrated conditional
ratio** r(z, D) = Σ actual_solar / Σ fc_solar over the trailing window
[D−32, D−2), restricted to hours with fc_share ≥ 0.3 and fc > 200 MW,
clamped to **[0.6, 1.0]** (never scales up; missing window ⇒ 1.0 = no-op).
In hours of day D with fc_share ≥ 0.3 and fc > 200, the solar component of
the renewable input is scaled by r via the first-class `renewable_modifier`
scenario hook (subtract (1−r)·solar_fc(h)). Strictly ex-ante (actuals lag
≤ D−2; the same vintage discipline as every trailing input). One reused
threshold (0.3); no new fitted parameters — r is measured, not tuned.

## Frozen evaluation (year scale from the start — the TR/MK lesson)

- **Arms**: baseline = `trmk_base_fy` (cv31 all-off, 2025-08-01..2026-07-31,
  this extract); treatment = same code + the debias scenario
  (`see_debias_fy`), same pipeline, same extract.
- **Primary gates**: (a) false-collapse hours (sim ≤ 20 & settled ≥ 60)
  summed over GR+BG+RO reduced **≥ 40%**; (b) each target zone's year MAE
  improves (any worsening > 0.1 fails).
- **Guards**: footprint MAE not worse than +0.05; per-zone envelope
  ΔMAE ≤ +3.0 / Δcorr ≥ −0.05 on all 39; ZERO new cap hours.
- **Report (no gate)**: within-regime (share ≥ 0.3) bias/MAE per zone;
  monthly deltas; collapse hit/false-alarm on the ≤5 threshold.
- **Interpretation guard**: single-month flips with arm-diffs at cap scale
  are counted separately (the measured degenerate-branch class, TR/MK
  results §full-year) and cannot carry a gate on their own.

## Disclosed

- "Actual" distributed solar in SEE is partly estimated by TSOs; if actuals
  undercount, r over-shrinks. The €89–100 ≈ gas-SRMC settled level in the
  same hours independently corroborates the direction and rough size.
- The weather track's ML solar (trained on actuals) should learn this bias
  implicitly; this treatment is for the RECORD path (announced inputs). The
  announced TRACK on the site stays verbatim-TSO by definition — the debias
  applies to the record/product counterfactual, not the announced-track
  display.
- Scope is the three measured zones only; other zones' ratios are future
  cartography, not silently included.
