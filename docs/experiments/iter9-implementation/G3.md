# Iteration-9 — G3 (final gate): 43-zone vs 39-zone scoring A/B — **NO-SHIP**

Gate G3 of the iter9 endogenization (AL / MK / ME / HR, 39 → 43 zones). See
`README.md` for the design and G1/G2. G3 asks the two questions that decide the
ship: **do the four new zones clear against their realized DA prices**, and
**does adding them perturb the 39-zone product** (the deliverable).

**Verdict: DO NOT SHIP.** Gate 4 (SEE bit-identity) passes; **Gates 1, 2 and 3
all fail.** Two of the four new zones (ME, and marginally AL) are mis-priced,
and — more decisively — adding the four zones **degrades the 39-zone product**,
including HU (the pre-registered control) by ΔMAE +11.4 €/MWh in July. HR and MK
clear cleanly; the failure is specific and diagnosed below with a concrete fix.

## Method (identical-conditions A/B)

Both arms ran through the SAME driver (`ab_run.jl`) against the SAME read-only
DuckDB extract, so the only difference between them is the four added zones and
their network plumbing — no backend/flow/solver confound.

- **Arm A (control):** `ZONESET=39` — the shipped 39-zone EU footprint.
- **Arm B (treatment):** `ZONESET=43` — FOOTPRINT39 + {AL, HR, ME, MK}.
- **Backend:** a windowed 43-zone DuckDB extract
  (`START_DATE=2026-03-01 END_DATE=2026-07-21 AGEN_BACK_DAYS=90
  AUX_BACK_DAYS=400`). The 400-day aux window carries a full trailing year of
  `physical_flows` + `day_ahead_total_load_forecast` (back to 2025-01-25) so the
  cv19 `:v3` (anad2) load-analogue flow pool is fully populated — the extract is
  not flow-starved. Both arms open it `EUPHEMIA_DUCKDB_READONLY=true` (shared
  read-only, no lock confound). Logs confirm `Ex-ante flow mode: v3` on both.
- **Clearing:** `run_multi_zone_market_clearing(d; order_method=:merit_order,
  enrich_network=true, passes=2, save_to_db=false)`, `OPTIMIZER=highs`, canonical
  period-decomposed reconstruction (default). Prices to TSV; no Postgres writes.
- **Flow rule:** cv19 `:v3` default on both arms (identical), so the A/B isolates
  zone addition, not the flow model.
- **Windows** (standard pattern, trimmed to the extract span + realized-price
  availability): `apr` 2026-04-01..05, `jul` 2026-07-06..21, `mar`
  2026-03-01..08 — **29 market days**, complete on both arms (39 and 43 zones ×
  29 days). New-zone realized-price coverage verified up front: AL/ME/MK hourly,
  HR quarter-hourly, all four full across every window.
- **Scoring** (`score_ab.jl` + `gate_check.jl`): resolution-aware hourly actuals
  from `entsoe.energy_prices` (iteration-4 standard: PT15M/PT30M hourly-meaned,
  duplicate `sequence` deduped), per-zone per-window corr / MAE / bias for both
  arms, plus the pre-registered gate verdicts. Raw table in `ab_scores.tsv`.

Execution note: the run was sharded (4 day-shards × 2 arms, concurrent read-only)
to fit wall-clock. HiGHS segfaulted (SIGSEGV in `HighsMipSolver::cleanupSolve` /
`HighsProfiling`) on 2 of ~60 day-solves — a known-flaky C-level crash in the
per-period decomposition, not a data problem; both days were re-run and
completed. This intermittent HiGHS instability is itself worth noting for the
backfill runner (a segfault bypasses the MPCC robustness ladder).

## Pre-registered gates (not weakened)

1. New zones clear: **corr ≥ 0.55** for MK/ME/HR, **≥ 0.40** for AL.
2. No existing (39) zone degrades by **> 0.03 corr** or **> 1.5 MAE**.
3. **HU control** (most exposed to the new SE border set) must not degrade on July.
4. **SEE guard:** GR single-zone stays bit-identical.

## Gate 4 — SEE guard: **PASS**

GR single-zone merit prices, this branch vs the fork point `077c7da`, both on the
extract, 2026-04-03: **bit-identical, 96/96 prices, 0 differing lines**
(`gr_single.jl`, `diff gr_iter9.tsv gr_ref.tsv`). The single-zone product is
untouched — confirmed numerically. (G1/G2 already held; this re-asserts cheaply.)

## Gate 1 — new zones clear: **FAIL** (ME fails; AL marginal)

Pooled corr over all 29 days (floors in brackets):

| zone | corr | floor | apr | jul | mar | bias | verdict |
|------|------|-------|-----|-----|-----|------|---------|
| HR   | **0.863** | 0.55 | 0.86 | 0.85 | 0.89 | −2.6 | **PASS** |
| MK   | **0.878** | 0.55 | 0.86 | 0.87 | 0.89 | +2.8 | **PASS** |
| AL   | 0.435 | 0.40 | 0.81 | **0.20** | 0.58 | +36.3 | marginal |
| ME   | **0.402** | 0.55 | 0.89 | **0.09** | 0.61 | +31.5 | **FAIL** |

HR and MK clear comfortably in every window. **AL and ME are fine in April

bias overall (model too high by ~€31–36). ME fails the 0.55 floor outright; AL
only clears its lower 0.40 floor on the pooled number while its July is 0.20.

## Gate 3 — HU control (July): **FAIL**

HU July: control corr 0.68 / MAE 72.4 → treatment corr 0.64 / MAE **83.8**
(**Δcorr −0.044, ΔMAE +11.4**). HU — flagged in advance as the zone most exposed
to the new SE border set — is the single worst-degraded existing zone.

## Diagnosis (which zone, which mechanism, one fix each)

**(A) Gate 1 — AL/ME mis-profiled.** AL (1.5 GW reservoir + 240 MW solar) and ME
(hydro + the Monita import cable) are **hydro/import-priced zones mis-assigned
the gas-anchored `SEE_PROFILE`**. The signature is textbook: in the hydro-rich
shoulder (March) they clear far *below* the coupled gas level (realized AL €86,
ME €69) while the model floors them at the SEE gas SRMC (~€125 → bias +42/+56);
in the July peak they *decouple* from the model's scarcity pricing (corr → 0.20 /
0.09, model overprices +36/+27). The control cases prove it: **MK — a real
lignite/gas/transit SEE zone — passes at 0.88 on the same `SEE_PROFILE`, and HR
passes at 0.86 precisely because it got the hydro-anchored `CROATIA_PROFILE`
(`opportunity_anchor=:hydro` + `import_backstop`).**
*Fix hypothesis:* reassign **AL and ME to a hydro-anchored profile** — the
`SLOVENIA/CROATIA` shape (`opportunity_anchor=:hydro`, `import_backstop=true`,
and for AL/ME a `backstop_scarcity_credit` like RS/RO/HU) — so their price
tracks the coupled water/import level instead of the gas SRMC. This directly
mirrors why HR succeeds and AL/ME fail.

**(B) Gate 2/3 — coupled-footprint perturbation of the 39.** Endogenizing the SE
zones and dropping the HR–HU / HR–SI Intraday-only borders **shifts the two-pass
opportunity-anchor propagation across the footprint**, and that leaks — non-
locally — into HU (July ΔMAE +11.4), DK1 (July Δcorr −0.074, far from the new
zones), SK, and a cluster of July Baltic/Nordic MAEs. This is the **same strong
non-local coupled-interaction mechanism cv18 documented** (PR #162): a change
that looks local ripples through the coupled clear. HU is the loudest because its
import neighbourhood (HR–HU dropped → observed imports restored; RS re-shaped by
the new HR–RS/MK–RS/ME–RS endogenous borders) moved most.
*Fix hypothesis:* the endogenization needs to be **border-scoped and validated on
the coupled footprint**, not zone-added wholesale — e.g. prevent the new SE
zones' coupling from entering HU's/DK1's pass-2 anchor references (an
anchor-inclusion scope like the `anchor_include_dropped` lever), and re-tune the
HR–HU / MK–BG treatment against the July HU regime specifically. A 2-zone pilot
cannot see this; the G-gates on the full footprint did exactly their job.

## What passes, and a possible narrower path

HR (CROATIA_PROFILE) and MK (SEE_PROFILE) both clear all three windows at
0.85–0.89 with small bias and do **not** drive the worst 39-zone regressions.
The blocking failures are (a) AL/ME needing the hydro-anchor rework and (b) the
coupled HU/DK1 degradation from the border set as a whole. Whether a narrower
41-zone step (add HR+MK, hold AL+ME) clears Gate 2/3 is a scoping decision for
the coordinator — this A/B measured the full 43 and it does not pass.

  AGEN_BACK_DAYS=90 AUX_BACK_DAYS=400 \
  OUT=data/extracts/euphemia_2026_iter9_43.duckdb \
  julia --project=. bin/build_duckdb_extract.jl

bash docs/experiments/iter9-implementation/run_g3_sharded.sh \
  data/extracts/euphemia_2026_iter9_43.duckdb /tmp/scratch

# 3. score + gate verdicts (actuals from Postgres)

  docs/experiments/iter9-implementation/ab_39.tsv \
  docs/experiments/iter9-implementation/ab_43.tsv \
  docs/experiments/iter9-implementation/ab_scores.tsv

  docs/experiments/iter9-implementation/ab_scores.tsv
