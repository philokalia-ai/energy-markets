# Bug sweep, second pass (2026-08-25)

Continuation of [bug-sweep-2026-08-24.md](bug-sweep-2026-08-24.md) after #342
merged: the items from its *Deferred* list that are unambiguous bugs, not design

## Fixed

**Physics (price-affecting)**

1. **Fleet completion no longer undoes a fresh outage** (`book_build.jl`). The
   completion target (trailing-30d p95 or installed) was measured with the
   outaged units running, so a fresh > 100 MW outage was re-added as an `AGG-`
   unit at SRMC until it had persisted ~30 days. The capacity the outage filter
   removed today (unfiltered registry − filtered, per type) is now subtracted
   from the gap; a gap the outage explains is logged (`⏸`) and not filled.
2. **Installed capacity by type is validity-filtered and day-keyed**
   (`fleet_data.jl`): units with `valid_from` after the day were counted
   (Flamanville 3 in FR's 2023 nuclear denominator, a CZ pumped-storage unit
   with `valid_from` 2027 counted today) — lookahead in the `:installed` fleet
   truth and the FR nuclear availability share. Callers pass `day`.
3. **`can_carry_flow` records both orientations** (`multi_zone_books.jl`): a
   border whose two directions are published on disjoint hours was judged
   unable to carry flow while the MPCC still created its flow variable — the
   border's energy was counted twice (observed injections kept + endogenous
   flow).
4. **Import-backstop headroom ignores lagged weeks with no observation**
   (`flows_imports.jl`): a missing week read as 0 net flow, which for a net
   exporter became "demonstrated import headroom = −climatology".
5. **cv32 wind delta sums per-type hourly means** (`book_build.jl`) instead of
   averaging Wind Onshore and Offshore together (would have added ~½ the
   total wind as a phantom delta the day DK1's package is enabled).
6. **Reservoir-dryness neighbourhood keeps ISO week 53** (`fleet_data.jl`).
7. **Gurobi retry rung keeps the caller's time budget** (`solver.jl`): rung 1
   silently cut 900 → 300 s, so a false INFEASIBLE could return as a poor
   `:time_limit` incumbent that callers accept.

**Plumbing / integrity**

8. **A read-only DuckDB process can read results** (`duckdb_store.jl`):
   `_ensure_results_attached` asserted writability on every read, so a
   parallel worker's `pipeline_day_saved` threw, was swallowed as "not
   saved", and already-saved days were re-cleared. Read-only processes now
   attach `results_db` READ_ONLY; writers still assert.
9. **A fleet built during a transient TTF/EUA lookup failure is not
   memoized** (`fuel_costs.jl`, `registry.jl`): one failed query used to pin
   every gas unit of that (zone, day) at the stylized fallback cost for the
   process lifetime, including the pass-2 rebuild.
10. **Orders outside the book's periods are refused** (`MPCC.jl`) instead of
    being folded into period 1; **malformed timeslots throw**
    (`OrderBookResult.jl`) instead of mapping to 00:00.
11. **Single-zone `optimization_runs` rows carry the solver that ran**
    (`single_zone.jl`), not the requested string (`"auto"` split the same
    configuration across two unique keys).
12. **`run_retro` bounds `RETRO_ASOF` to realized days** (a retro row for a
    future day would collide with the live write's UNIQUE key); a manual
    `pregate` dispatch honours `force_rerun` (workflow).
13. `ZoneProfile.input_corrections` gets its PROVENANCE entry (methodology
    export test).

**Tests**

14. `test_zone_profiles.jl` and `test_zone_strategy_export.jl` rewritten to
    the current registry (cv21 Viking on DK1, cv22 UA books on RO/HU/SK/PL,
    cv23 NO1/NO3 anchored + FR_PROFILE, cv25 T1/T3 applied at resolution time,
    cv32 islands) — they had asserted `===` against the pre-cv21 constants
    since July and rotted into 13 permanent failures.
