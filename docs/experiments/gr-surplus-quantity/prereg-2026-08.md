# GR surplus-quantity package — PREREG (2026-08-09; frozen at merge, before any scored run)

## Evidence base (measured, closed experiments)

- Market-implied (PR #320): in GR settled-≤5€ hours the single-zone book needs
  +2.5 GW solar vs actuals (median +689 MW vs fc, p75 +1.4 GW) before its own
  price collapses; in **304 of 1,083 collapse hours (28%) it cannot reach ≤5€
  at ALL** (even at 1.3×cap95 solar the marginal tranche stays above) — the
  hard core of the deficit is quantity/structure, not input accuracy.
- R1 (PR #321): GR solar input winner exists (ML −48%) but is quarantined by
  co-adaptation; the collapse residual survives even oracle inputs.
- Collapse seasonality: Apr–Jun dominates (176/206/140 regime-hours), plus an
  Aug–Sep shoulder.

## Hypothesis

In genuine surplus hours the REAL market clears at/below zero because
inflexible supply (must-run thermal/hydro, contracted imports) does NOT
withdraw, while OUR book prices those deep blocks above the collapse range —
so the model's floor is structurally too high. The fix is quantity-side:
surplus-conditional deepening of the blocks that should not yield, so the
marginal tranche in surplus hours becomes the RES/floor block.

## Candidate levers (each an isolated switch; leave-one-out attribution)

1. **GR in-regime negative floor**: the shipped cv31 mechanism with GR added
   to the zone list (RES block + RoR + deepest must-run price at −20 in
   regime hours). Sized for bucket A. A/B needs no new code — the
   `EUPHEMIA_SOLAR_REGIME_ZONES` override is the treatment arm; shipping it
   means adding GR to the scoped default under this package's switch.
2. **Surplus-valley continuation tranche** (bucket B, the cycling/commitment
   deficit): thermal units with demonstrated overnight running offer their
   committed MW at the floor during valley hours, replacing their SRMC price
   for that quantity only (gate + sizing in "Regime gates" below). New
   mechanism in the GR book path behind its own switch.
3. NOTHING else moves (no scarcity, no export-absorption steps, no import
   reshaping — the cv18 non-local-coupling lesson keeps border-shaped levers
   out of a single-zone package).

## Regime gates (ex-ante, FROZEN after the Phase-0 census)

- **Lever 1 (floor)**: the cv31 gate unchanged — day-ahead solar share of
  forecast load ≥ θ=0.4, GR added to the zone list. Measured coverage: the
  gate fires on 93/93 bucket-A hours (floor_diag 2026-08-09; the book then
  reaches −20, price moved on 93/93).
- **Lever 2 (valley continuation)**: hours 04–13 UTC with day-ahead solar
  share of forecast load ≥ 0.25 (the bucket-B morning ramp: 07–11 UTC holds
  165/211 hours), AND per-unit trailing evidence: a thermal unit offers its
  continuation tranche only if its trailing-28d actual output in hours
  00–04 UTC was > 10% of p_max on ≥ 60% of days (ex-ante overnight-runner
  test, 2-day lag). Quantity = trailing-28d p25 of its 00–04 UTC output
  (the demonstrated committed MW); price = the declared floor. One new
  declared parameter beyond cv31's θ: the 0.25 valley share threshold.

## Phase 0 census results (2026-08-09, census_phase0.csv)

Pricing every collapse day at 1.3×cap95 solar splits the 304 unreachable
hours into two distinct pathologies:

- **Bucket A, floor depth (n=93)**: the book DOES collapse — to ~1€ (median
  p_maxsolar 1.0) — but the settled price is negative (median −6.5, p10
  −35.7; 88/93 hours settled <0). GR is not in the cv31 CONTINENTAL_SOLAR
  group, so its book has no negative-price capability: the deficit here is
  the FLOOR, not quantity. (The identified collapse hours confirm: when the
  book collapses it lands at median 1.0.)
- **Bucket B, stick-high (n=211)**: price sticks at median 17.1 (p90 80.0)
  even at max solar, settled median 0.0. Concentrated in MORNING ramp hours
  (07–11h UTC = 45+46+38+36 of the 211) and Feb/Mar/May — not midday. Some
  tranche priced in the teens (and a gas-SRMC tail near 80) refuses to
  yield; its identity (must-run discount / import injection / hydro) is the
  book-anatomy question below.

This reshapes the levers: lever 1 becomes the GR-scoped in-regime negative
floor (the cv31 mechanism extended to GR), sized for bucket A; lever 2
targets whatever the bucket-B fingerprint names, morning-gated.
