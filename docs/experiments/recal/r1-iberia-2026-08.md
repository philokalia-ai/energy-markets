## Verdict

The raw TSO forecast is the best available input on all four Iberian
zone-targets — decisively so in PT (fc corr ≥ 0.97 both targets). Iberia
joins NL in the "already best-served" class: **the Iberia package has no
input leg**, and per the R-cycle policy its mechanism recalibration waits
for leave-one-out evidence rather than input-driven co-adaptation packages.
No correction series are emitted for ES/PT.
