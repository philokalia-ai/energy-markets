# Nuclear recent-silence derate — preregistration (FROZEN 2026-08-08 ~15:00Z, before the scored run)

**Motivation (measured).** The Paks/Danube episode (owner-flagged, 2026-08-05):
MVM's rolling day-by-day outage filings publish ~20:00Z of the eve — after
every forecast build — so the HU book carried 1,916 MW of dark nuclear
(HU lead-1 MAE 113/75 on the gap days). The year census generalizes it:
**591 unit-days** (2025-08..2026-07) of a nuclear unit ≥300 MW that was dark
(< 5% p_max) for the trailing 72 h, demonstrably alive in the trailing 30 d
(> 20% p_max), with **no Active filing covering the delivery day published
by D−1 ~16:00Z** — and the darkness persisted into the delivery day in
**~90%** of them: SE3 262/272 (96%), FR 129/149 (87%, avg 1,072 MW), CH
34/44, ES 38/42, CZ 27/27, RO 20/21, SI 16/17, NL 11/15.

**The named characteristic.** A nuclear unit that is dark is never
economically withheld — darkness is unavailability, filed or not. The rule:
for delivery day D, a unit with (registry Nuclear, p_max ≥ 300) AND
trailing-72h max output < 5% p_max AND trailing-30d max > 20% p_max AND no
Active outage row covering D known at build time ⇒ a SYNTHETIC outage entry
at available_capacity = trailing-72h max (≈ exclusion), flowing through the
existing outage-application path (conservative MIN semantics). Strictly
ex-ante: only past actuals and already-published filings. Mirror of the
existing stale-outage override (which handles "generating despite filed
out").

**Implementation.** Inside the day-level outage computation
(`get_day_outages`), gated **opt-in** `EUPHEMIA_ENABLE_NUKE_SILENCE` for the
A/B (default absent ⇒ byte-identical; ship decision and its default/kill
switch come after the numbers).

## Frozen evaluation (year scale)

- Arms: baseline `trmk_base_fy`; treatment same + switch ON, label
  `nuke_silence_fy`, same extract/pipeline.
- **Primary**: FR and SE3 year MAE each improve ≥ 0.2.
- **Guards**: no zone worse than +0.3 MAE / −0.02 corr; **ZERO new cap
  hours** (the 10% wrong-derate days remove real capacity — the rule fails
  if that manufactures scarcity); footprint MAE not worse than +0.05.
- Report: per-zone deltas, the wrong-derate days' price cost, HU gap-day
  case study (2026-08-06/07 out of window — noted qualitatively).
- Degenerate-flip interpretation guard applies (single-month cap-scale
  flips counted separately).

## Amendment 1 (2026-08-08 ~15:25Z, BEFORE the scored run)

The backfill-visible population differs from the live one: on historical
days the outage table already contains the late filings (the record path's
known filing-lookahead), so the build-time-invisible FR/CH/ES events vanish
from a year A/B. The **never-filed** subset — what a backfill can actually
see — is almost entirely **SE3: 276 unit-days, 92% persistence** (likely
including asset-code mismatches between Swedish filings and the registry,
which our loader can never apply — a standing book defect this rule also
fixes). Primary gate revised accordingly: **SE3 year MAE improves ≥ 0.2**
(FR dropped from the primary — it is a LIVE-path beneficiary, measured
qualitatively on forward days). All other gates unchanged.
